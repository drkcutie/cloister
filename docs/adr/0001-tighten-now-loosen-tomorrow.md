# Tighten now, loosen tomorrow

Every setting that limits you (Blocklists, Shutdown Time, Workday Start, Workdays, the weekly Deep Hours target) applies a change that makes it stricter **immediately**. A change that makes it looser only applies from the **next day**, or the next week for weekly settings. The point of the app is to bind your future self. Any loosening that applied immediately would let you undo a commitment in the very moment you're tempted, so we accept the inconvenience of waiting. Clients and the backend both enforce this, and the backend's check is the one that counts.
