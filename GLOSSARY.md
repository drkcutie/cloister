# Cloister

A cross-device blocker built on Cal Newport's Deep Work. It makes focused work unavoidable, and once the workday ends it makes not working unavoidable too.

## Language

### Blocking

**Blocklist**:
A named set of domains and apps that are blocked together, such as "Social" or "News". The **Distraction List** and **Work List** are the two built-in kinds.
_Avoid_: Filter, rules, deny list

**Distraction List**:
The default Blocklists covering social, news, video, shopping and forums. They are on unless you opt out.
_Avoid_: Bad sites

**Work List**:
A Blocklist of work tools, such as chat, email and code hosting. It is blocked only during **Evening Lockdown** and on **Rest Days**.
_Avoid_: Work sites

**Block**:
A period during which one or more Blocklists are enforced on every Device. It can't be shortened or cancelled once it has started. Starting one means choosing whether it is a **Deep Session**, which is the default.
_Avoid_: Session (on its own), lock, timer

**Deep Session**:
A **Block** that also has an **Intent** and a **Depth Rating**. Only Deep Sessions count toward **Deep Hours**.
_Avoid_: Focus session, pomodoro

**Device**:
A computer or phone, signed in to your account, that enforces Blocks.
_Avoid_: Client, machine

**Hardcore Mode**:
An opt-in Android setup in which turning off enforcement is impossible, not just detected. Desktops have no Hardcore Mode: their strictness is SelfControl-level.
_Avoid_: Strict mode, lockdown mode

**Breach**:
A recorded event in which a Device's enforcement was disabled or bypassed during a Block.
_Avoid_: Violation, cheat

**Quit-Social Block**:
A 30-day **Block** covering only the Social Distraction List. You start it deliberately, as an experiment in going without social media.
_Avoid_: Sabbath, detox

### Planning

**Day Plan**:
A single day divided into **Slots** from **Workday Start** to **Shutdown Time**. It is written during the previous evening's **Shutdown Ritual**.
_Avoid_: Schedule, agenda, timetable

**Slot**:
A labelled stretch of time on a **Day Plan**. A deep Slot becomes a **Deep Session**, and any other Slot is only a label.
_Avoid_: Time block, block, event

**Schedule**:
A recurring template, such as "weekdays 08:00–11:00 deep", that fills in the draft **Day Plan** during the **Shutdown Ritual**.
_Avoid_: Recurring block, routine

**Planned Deep Minutes**:
The total length of deep Slots on a day. It only goes up: adding a deep Slot raises it, and nothing lowers it. Slots can move to other times.
_Avoid_: Deep quota

**Workday**:
A configured day of the week that has a **Day Plan** and a **Shutdown Ritual**. Any other day is a **Rest Day**.
_Avoid_: Weekday, work day

**Rest Day**:
A day that isn't a **Workday**. The **Work List** and **Distraction List** are blocked all day.
_Avoid_: Weekend, day off

**Workday Start**:
The configured time when **Evening Lockdown** ends on a **Workday**. Changes to it take effect the next day.
_Avoid_: Morning, start time

### Shutdown

**Shutdown Time**:
The time of day when the **Shutdown Ritual** becomes due. Changes to it take effect the next day.
_Avoid_: End of day, cutoff

**Shutdown Ritual**:
A required end-of-day sequence: capture loose ends, review today, check what's coming, plan tomorrow, then type "Shutdown complete". It becomes due at **Shutdown Time** and takes over every Device's screen until it is completed or becomes a **Missed Ritual**.
_Avoid_: End session, closing routine

**Shutdown Delay**:
A single 30-minute postponement of the **Shutdown Ritual**, limited to one per week and always logged.
_Avoid_: Snooze, skip, escape

**Evening Lockdown**:
The period from **Shutdown Time** until the next **Workday Start**, governed only by the clock. During it, the **Work List** is blocked as well as the **Distraction List**, and no Deep Session can start.
_Avoid_: Night mode, off hours

**Missed Ritual**:
A **Shutdown Ritual** not completed by the next **Workday Start**. It lapses and the next day runs on its **Schedule**. It is recorded for the weekly review.
_Avoid_: Overdue ritual, skipped ritual

### Reflection

**Loose Ends**:
A running list of open tasks and commitments, captured during the **Shutdown Ritual** so they stay out of your head overnight.
_Avoid_: Todo list, inbox, backlog

**Intent**:
A one-line statement, written before focused work begins, of what that work will produce.
_Avoid_: Goal, task, note

**Depth Rating**:
Your own rating of how deep a stretch of focused work was, recorded when it ends.
_Avoid_: Score, focus score

**Weekly Review**:
A required extra step in the last **Workday**'s **Shutdown Ritual** of each week. It covers the week's **Deep Hours**, Depth Ratings, **Missed Rituals**, **Shutdown Delays** and **Breaches**, and sets next week's target.
_Avoid_: Weekly report, stats, retro

**Deep Hours**:
The total length of **Deep Sessions** over a period, planned or unplanned and whatever their **Depth Rating**, tracked against a weekly target.
_Avoid_: Focus time, productive hours
